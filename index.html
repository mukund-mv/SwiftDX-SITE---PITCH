Why hospitals would pay

SwiftDx helps hospitals reduce expensive re-operations, minimise pathology bottlenecks, and improve intraoperative surgical decision-making.

One avoided re-excision can offset months of software subscription cost, creating a strong and measurable hospital ROI case.

Pricing strategy

SwiftDx intentionally enters the market with accessible pricing to maximise adoption and demonstrate fast ROI.

The strategy is land-and-expand: begin with pilot deployments and usage-based pricing, then scale into annual enterprise contracts across hospital networks.

Who makes the buying decision?

Hospital administration and finance teams ultimately control purchasing decisions.

Pathologists and surgeons drive adoption internally, but procurement depends on demonstrated workflow impact and economic ROI.

Why partner with companies like Philips or Roche?

These companies already own large parts of the digital pathology workflow and hospital infrastructure.

Integration into existing systems lowers adoption friction, accelerates deployment, and enables faster enterprise-scale expansion.
